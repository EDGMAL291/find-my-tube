Clinical reference support

Find My Tube is a mobile-first reference tool for clinical teams. It supports test-to-tube matching, suggested test exploration from symptoms and clinical concerns, tube planning before collection, and consumables ordering and tracking workflows.

It is intended to support safer, faster specimen preparation in busy clinical settings. It is not a diagnostic authority and does not replace local protocol or clinical judgement.

Always confirm urgent, paediatric, transfusion, and site-specific requirements with local protocol or senior clinical/laboratory review.
