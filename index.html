Collection checklist

  1. Confirm identity. Check the patient against your local identification process before collection.
  2. Label at the patient. Apply labels immediately after collection, never in advance.
  3. Use the right tube. Check the test requirement, fill line, and additive before drawing.
  4. Handle to protocol. Mix, transport, and prioritise samples according to your local laboratory SOP.
